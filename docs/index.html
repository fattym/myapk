MyBidhaa Privacy Policy

App: MyBidhaa – School Books & Supplies Kenya · Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

MyBidhaa ("we", "us", "our") sells school books and supplies in Kenya through the MyBidhaa mobile app and mybidhaa.com. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written to comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Data we collect

DataWhen
Full name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed)When you register or sign in
Delivery address, town/area and location details you enterWhen you place an order or save an address
Order history, cart, wishlist, school and class selectionsWhen you shop
Booklist photos or files (images, PDFs)When you scan or upload a booklist. Scanning reads text on your device; a file is sent to us only if you choose to upload or send it.
Messages and order details you send to us via WhatsAppWhen you tap a WhatsApp button (handled by WhatsApp under its own policy)
Payment details such as the M-Pesa phone number and transaction referenceWhen you pay. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or card numbers.
Device and usage data (app version, device type, error and connection information, IP address)Automatically, to keep the app working and secure

The app uses your device camera or photo library only when you choose to scan or pick a booklist, and only with your permission.

3. How we use your data

  • Create and manage your customer account and sign you in.
  • Process, deliver and support your orders, including contacting you about them.
  • Take payments (including M-Pesa) and issue receipts.
  • Match booklists to products and prepare quotes.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and improve the app.
  • Meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only where needed to serve you:

  • Payment providers (e.g. M-Pesa/Safaricom) to process payments.
  • Delivery and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Hosting and technical service providers that run our servers and app.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you choose to contact us or send an order through it.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

These parties may use your data only to provide their service to us.

5. Security and retention

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, and sign-in uses secure token authentication.
  • Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it.

We keep account data while your account is active. Order and payment records are kept as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes (generally up to 7 years). Uploaded booklists are kept only as long as needed to prepare your quote and handle your order. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

6. Your rights and account deletion

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Update or correct it (you can edit profile and address details in the app's Account section).
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To make a request, contact us using the details below from the phone number or email linked to your account. We will respond within 30 days. Some records may be retained where the law requires. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).

7. Children

The app sells school items and is intended for parents, guardians and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without a parent or guardian's involvement.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
